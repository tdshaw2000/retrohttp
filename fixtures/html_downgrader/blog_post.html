Debugging a Weird Memory Leak 🐛

Posted by J. Vortex on 2026-08-14

Last week I spent way too long chasing a leak that only showed up after ~48 hours of uptime. Here’s what I found…

The naive fix was to just restart the process nightly, but that felt like giving up. So I dug in with a heap profiler.

heap.dump('snapshot-1.heapsnapshot');
setTimeout(() => heap.dump('snapshot-2.heapsnapshot'), 1000 * 60 * 60);

Turns out a cache of parsed templates was never being evicted. Classic — a Map used as a cache with no TTL and no size bound.
