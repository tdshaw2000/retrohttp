Council Approves Waterfront Redevelopment

By Françoise Duval — Staff Writer

The city council voted 6–1 on Tuesday night to approve a “transformative” redevelopment of the downtown waterfront, clearing the way for a $40 million mixed-use project.

Councilmember Renée Aubert called the vote “a turning point for the district” and said construction could begin as early as next spring…
